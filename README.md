# KaPosts

KaPosts is an iOS app for the on-chain social feed and public chat rooms on the Kaspa BlockDAG.
Every post, reply, like, follow and room message is a Kaspa transaction from your own address.

- **Feed** - Following, Feed and Popular, with replies, reposts, quotes, polls and tips
- **Public Chats** - open rooms such as #kaspa, plus rooms in other languages
- **Profile** - your posts, replies, followers and the profile everyone sees
- **Wallet** - your address's balance and transaction history
- **Settings** - security, connection, notifications, KaPosts and Public Chats options

One account is one address: create a new seed phrase or import an existing one (a KaChat seed
gives you the same identity and posts as in KaChat).
